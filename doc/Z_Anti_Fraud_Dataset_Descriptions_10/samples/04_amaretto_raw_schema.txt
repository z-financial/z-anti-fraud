Amaretto raw schema (12 attributes documented by the dataset/benchmark):
- Transaction ID
- Originator
- Originator_ID
- EntryDate
- Normalized Amount
- InputOutput
- Market
- Product ISIN
- Product Type
- Product Class
- Currency
- Anomaly

Important:
- Anomaly = 0 is normal; positive values distinguish one of five injected anomaly classes.
- The CSV example in 04_amaretto_published_paper_example.csv is an illustrative example
  published in the Amaretto paper figure. It was NOT extracted from the multipart archive.
- The official dataset archive is split into 34 binary ZIP parts in the official repository.
